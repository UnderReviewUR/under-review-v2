How to read NFL player props before kickoff

A short checklist — not a lock sheet. Use it on the board you can actually bet, then ask Under Review for a structured take on the posted line.

1. Start from the posted main

Ignore alt ladders and period scraps. Read the half-point skill main your book actually prices (rush yards, receiving yards, receptions, pass yards). If there is no honest posted line, the correct move is often to pass.

2. Confirm the player is on the right team

Roster moves wreck stale priors. Before you lean over or under, make sure the name still belongs to the offense you think you’re betting — then check role (starter vs depth) against that matchup.

3. Ask usage, not vibes

  • Volume: targets, carries, or dropbacks that support the line
  • Script risk: blowout or negative game flow that kills the path
  • Competition: other mouths in the same offense eating the same pie

4. Treat “pass” as a take

No posted GOAT-tier main, unclear role, or only a special-teams scrap? Passing is sharper than forcing a number. Under Review is built to say that out loud.
